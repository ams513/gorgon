# Australia: Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles alignment checklist

> **Informational, not legal advice. Not a certification and not a compliance claim.** State and territory
> health-records laws (e.g. Health Records Act 2001 (Vic), Health Records and Information Privacy Act 2002
> (NSW)) may also apply. Recent amendments (Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024) add obligations
> on a staged timetable - check which are in force. Have a qualified lawyer review this.

Jurisdiction profile: `au_privacy` (consent age default 15, the OAIC's general presumption; assess capacity).

| APP / requirement | Feature that supports it | What the CLINIC must still do |
|---|---|---|
| APP 1 Open and transparent management | Privacy notice template; plain-language explanations | Publish an APP privacy policy; keep it current |
| APP 2 Anonymity and pseudonymity | Pseudonymous ids in audit and tombstones | Offer anonymity where practicable (rarely practicable for care) |
| APP 3 Collection; sensitive information needs consent | Opt-in per purpose with signed receipts; fixed field lists | Collect only what is reasonably necessary |
| APP 5 Notification of collection | Explanations name who, why, how long | Give an APP 5 notice at or before collection |
| APP 6 Use or disclosure | Purpose-limited reads; named partners only | Use only for the primary purpose or a permitted secondary purpose |
| APP 7 Direct marketing | No marketing purpose exists; reminders are opt-in | Do not use health information for marketing without consent |
| APP 8 Cross-border disclosure | Nothing leaves the PC by default; no outside AI service | Assess any overseas vendor |
| APP 10 Quality | Editable preferences | Keep information accurate and up to date |
| APP 11 Security; destroy or de-identify when no longer needed | Encryption, roles, idle lock, audit; retention purge; erasure with tombstone | Physical security, training, tested backups; destruction of old backups |
| APP 12 Access | JSON + PDF export | Respond within a reasonable period (30 days is the usual benchmark) |
| APP 13 Correction | Editing and versioned entries | Correct on request; note disagreements |
| Notifiable Data Breaches (Part IIIC) | Audit integrity check; breach runbook | Assess within 30 days; notify the OAIC and affected people as soon as practicable |
| Automated decisions transparency (amendments; check commencement) | The assistant makes no decisions about people; clinician sign-off for clinical content | Describe the assistant in your privacy policy |
| My Health Records Act (if you connect to My Health Record) | Not connected by this software | Follow the MHR rules separately |
