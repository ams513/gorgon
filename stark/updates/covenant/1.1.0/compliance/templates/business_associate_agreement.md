# Business Associate Agreement

> **Template - have a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction review before use.** This is a starting outline that
> follows the elements in 45 CFR 164.504(e) and 164.314(a). It is not a complete contract. HHS publishes sample
> provisions; your counsel should adapt them to your state law and the vendor's actual services.

Note on Stark Covenant: the software runs on the clinic's own computer and its maker does not receive PHI from
a normal installation. Use this agreement with vendors that **do** create, receive, maintain or transmit PHI for
you (for example IT support with remote access, an off-site backup service, a message-sending service), and
with the software maker only if a support arrangement would give them access to PHI.

**Parties:** [COVERED ENTITY] ("Covered Entity") and [VENDOR] ("Business Associate"). Effective date: [DATE].

1. **Definitions.** Terms have the meanings in 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164.
2. **Permitted uses and disclosures.** Business Associate may use or disclose PHI only to perform [SERVICES], as
   required by law, or as otherwise permitted by this Agreement, and never in a way that would violate Subpart E
   if done by Covered Entity. Minimum necessary applies.
3. **Safeguards.** Business Associate will use appropriate administrative, physical and technical safeguards
   and comply with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164 for electronic PHI.
4. **Reporting.** Business Associate will report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure not provided for by
   this Agreement, any security incident, and any breach of unsecured PHI (45 CFR 164.410) without unreasonable
   delay and within [X] days of discovery, with the information Covered Entity needs to notify individuals.
5. **Subcontractors.** Business Associate will ensure any subcontractor that handles PHI agrees in writing to
   the same restrictions and conditions (164.502(e)(1)(ii), 164.308(b)(2)).
6. **Individual rights.** Business Associate will make PHI available for access (164.524), amendment (164.526)
   and an accounting of disclosures (164.528) within [X] days of Covered Entity's request.
7. **Covered Entity's obligations.** To the extent Business Associate carries out Covered Entity's obligations
   under Subpart E, it will comply with the requirements that apply to Covered Entity.
8. **Books and records.** Business Associate will make its internal practices, books and records available to
   the Secretary of HHS for determining compliance.
9. **Term and termination.** This Agreement lasts while Business Associate holds PHI. Covered Entity may
   terminate if Business Associate violates a material term and does not cure it within [X] days.
10. **Return or destruction.** On termination, Business Associate will return or destroy all PHI, or, if not
    feasible, extend these protections and limit further uses.
11. **Miscellaneous.** [Indemnification, insurance, governing law, amendment to follow changes in law,
    interpretation in favour of HIPAA compliance.]
