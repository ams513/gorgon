## Our AI assistant

Some messages are written with the help of an AI assistant that runs **only on the clinic's own computer**. It
does not send your information to any outside AI company. It always says it is an AI assistant, and you can
always reach a person by replying PERSON or calling [PHONE]. It never diagnoses, never changes or doses
medicines and never decides how urgent something is. Any medical reply is checked and signed by a clinician
before it reaches you. It uses your preferred name, language and tone only if you agree.

If you tell it you are in danger or thinking of harming yourself, it gives you emergency and crisis numbers and
tells your care team, so a person can follow up. [DESCRIBE YOUR CLINIC POLICY.]

role needs. Every view and change is recorded in a tamper-evident log. Backups are encrypted.
