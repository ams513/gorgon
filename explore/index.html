Six interactive pages on the speed of light, the mind, choice, nothingness, infinity and logic. Free, no account, nothing to install.

  • The Physics of Faster-Than-Light Travel

    Drag a slider toward the speed of light and watch time dilate. Explore warp bubbles, wormholes, and why distant galaxies already recede faster than light.

  • The Architecture of Mind

    Four lenses on the mind, layers of reality, quantum cognition and what may happen to consciousness, with a quiz on your own philosophy of mind.

  • The Quantum Architecture of Choice

    An interactive look at the mathematics of choice, from classical behavior to quantum cognition and universal agents, with a decision-space visualizer.

  • Nothing: A Journey Into the Void

    Eight ideas of nothingness, from Madhyamaka emptiness to Meillassoux's hyper-chaos. Apply the fourfold negation to any concept, then take the quiz.

  • Infinity Explorer

    Cantor's transfinite numbers, Godel's incompleteness theorems and Russell's paradox. Step through the diagonal argument yourself.

  • Beyond True and False

    Build truth tables, watch the liar paradox flip, and switch off the rules of classical logic to see which formal systems remain.
