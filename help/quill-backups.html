Stark Quill: Backups and moving computers

Quill saves as you type, backs up the whole book every couple of minutes and keeps every paragraph you delete. Your own copy is one folder away.

What Quill already does

  • Every save goes to a journal first and is checked before it replaces the chapter file.
  • Backups of each book are zip files in the .backups folder inside the book's folder.
  • Deleted paragraphs go to Graveyard and to Deleted_Graveyard.md in the book folder.

Get something back

Yesterday's version of a chapter
Open Backups, pick a backup, press Show chapters, then Preview and Restore this version. Quill backs up the current text first, so a restore can be undone too.
A whole chapter you deleted
Backups > Deleted chapters > Restore. It comes back at the end of the book; drag it where it belongs.
A paragraph you deleted
Open Graveyard, put the cursor where you want it and press Put back at the cursor.

Your own backup

Copy Documents\My_Books to a USB stick or another drive. Each chapter is a plain text file, so any text editor can open it, today or in twenty years.

Moving to a new computer

  1. Install Quill on the new computer (see Install).
  2. Copy your My_Books folder into Documents there.
  3. For Pro: a PRO download activates itself; a key from an email gets pasted under Licence. On the old computer, Licence > Turn Pro off on this PC.
