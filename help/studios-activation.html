Stark World Engine: Activation and keys

Stark World Engine works the moment you unzip it, with or without a key. A key registers your copy and turns on the Godot 4 and UE5 exporters of the Pro and Studio editions.

Activate in one minute

  1. After buying you receive one line of text that starts with GS1-. That is your key.
  2. Start the studio, open Export > Activate, paste the key and press Activate.

Or save the key as key.txt in the same folder as studio_app.py and restart the studio.

Good to know

  • No internet, no account, no server. The key is checked on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • A missing, mistyped or damaged key never stops the program. It shows "unregistered" and keeps running.
  • The licence is personal: use it on your own computers for your own projects, and please keep your download and key to yourself.

If activation shows an error

  • Paste the whole key in one piece. It is one long line starting with GS1-; email apps sometimes break long lines, so copy it from a plain-text view if you can.
  • No spaces or extra characters at the start or end.
  • Using key.txt? It must sit next to studio_app.py and contain only the key.

Godot / UE5 buttons are greyed out

They need a valid Pro or Studio key. The lines under the export buttons and next to Activate show your edition and whether you're registered. Only Pro and Studio keys include these exporters.

Lost your key, or want to upgrade?

Write to hello@starkforall.com with your itch.io order details. A lost key is re-issued offline; for an upgrade, tell me which edition you have and which you'd like.

Still stuck?

Write to hello@starkforall.com with your operating system, browser and edition, what the black window (or Terminal) printed, and doctor.log / launcher.log from the studio folder if you can. Please never post your licence key in a public comment.
